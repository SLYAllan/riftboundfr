Den Updated: May 18, 2026 Table of Contents 1 Play Style 2 Key Cards 3 Competitive Status 4 Best Performances in Unleashed 5 Irelia Decks The Noxian occupation of Ionia produced many heroes, none more unlikely than young Irelia of Navori. Trained in the ancient dances of her province, she adapted her art for war, using the graceful and carefully practised movements to levitate a host of deadly blades. After proving herself as a fighter, she was thrust into the role of resistance leader and figurehead, and to this day remains dedicated to the preservation of her homeland. Play Style Irelia is all about finding the right timing to start using her ability to push for points. Too early and we won't have enough runes to develop, but too late and the opponent has time to build their side of the board. Unleashed didn't bring any new ways to play the Blade Dancer, still very much focused on SFD-057 , but two great cards to interact with the opponent. Indeed, UNL-150 and UNL-042 allow to stun opposing units, and limit how many runes we have to keep open for reactive purposes. With the unit set or the spell hidden at a battlefield, it is much more difficult to threaten SFD-057 with large units. This leaves spells as the default option, something the Calm domain is stellar at countering. Key Cards Irelia is focused on extracting as much value as possible from UNL-150 , SFD-048 and SFD-057 . Thus, the deck will build around drawing and supporting these three cards in particular. We have all sorts or cards to do so, starting with our 2-costs UNL-053 and SFD-036 to fetch our key units early on. Once on the board, we can move them around with OGS-011 or SFD-133 . Keep them alive with SFD-051 or OGN-077 . Buff their might with OGN-058&nbsp; or SFD-196 . Counter spells with OGN-045 . Overall, Irelia is building around her own strategy first and foremost. Once you feel you have enough cards to reliably achieve that goal, cards like OGN-192 can be added to the mix for disruptive purposes. Competitive Status Irelia is a top tier legend early in Unleashed, both because the metagame is midrange oriented, which is perfect for her signature play style, but also due to the few reinforcements she got, improving her core gameplay even more. After her win in Sydney, the Blade Dancer even showed she can win against Aurora decks, a very difficult match-up, anchoring her position as one of the premier legends in Riftbound for Unleashed.
