There are tales of a terror… a monster that lurks; waiting to ambush unsuspecting competitors who come across his path. DSG Prismaticism won RQ LA with Rengar, defeating Xeno who was playing the event’s most popular deck: Irelia.

![](https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/images/dsfx7636/news_live/abfc0b707f45f5b9544feb3fcbc89b1f646bafe1-1920x1080.jpg)

The Top 8 for this event featured: 2 Irelia, 2 Azir, 1 Master YI, Wuju Bladesman, 1 Akali, 1 Ornn, and 1 Rengar. While it was DSG that had the winner, MICE was well represented with both THEMANLAND (Ornn) and Squirtle (Azir) representing them in the Top 8.

After the recent bans, Irelia has moved into the top spot, unseating Kennen as the most played deck of the event. Kennen undeniably remains a powerful deck, putting the most players into Day 2 of LA. However the lack of Kennen in the Top 8 also makes clear that players came into the event with plans for countering it. It was DSG Prismaticism’s teammate, DSG AwesomeRosum, who earned the Best-Of Kennen.
