Our penultimate Regional Qualifier took place this past weekend in Singapore with another exciting look at the metagame for Riftbound. The Top Eight saw three Legends: five Kennen, two Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman, and one Akali, which piloted by Gorica was able to take it all down and win RQ Singapore. As the most populous deck on Day 1, Day 2 and in the Top 8 there is no question that Kennen remains a powerful choice for players.

We are also seeing many of the same faces taking home their Champion’s Best-Of prize, a foil plated legend: RQ Barcelona’s victor MICE TheManLand won with Ornn again, CTG Alanzq with Mel, DSG Prismaticism with Rengar, MICE Squirtle as a two-time RQ winner won with Azir, V0LKATA2 repeated their best-of Draven, and Jibbs with Jhin.

Of the decks which performed above their percentage of the field when we compare Day 1 to Day 2, there are a few which saw their presence on the field increase, with Kennen, Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman, Azir, LeBlanc, and Jayce all increasing by at least two percent over their Day 1 percentages. With one final Regional Qualifier of the year left, RQ Los Angeles, September 25-27, let’s see how the dust will settle before we head into the end-of-year Regional Championships!
